How to Book a Group Travel on Singapore Airlines?

Group Travel Airlines

A tour arranged with Singapore Airlines opens doors to many exclusive features and services. The airline also allows groups to take advantage of its unique travel offerings. Here, larger parties seeking to travel together can easily schedule their flight. For such trips, premium Singapore Airlines group travel service is offered by the airline on its operated routes. However, it also requires groups to have the required number of passengers to initiate such a trip. Besides, this travel feature also has various other guidelines that need to be fulfilled in order to enjoy a comfortable voyage with a companion. By reading the outlined subheadings, they can explore all that information in detail.

Does Singapore Airlines Do Group Bookings?

Absolutely! Singapore Airlines does group bookings. But this exception travel offering is available for passengers seeking to fly in larger numbers together on the same flight. Furthermore, travelers have to comply with the Singapore Airlines group booking policy for a smoother travel experience. Those who are not aware of these policies may not be able to complete their reservation. Therefore, learn the rules of group booking on Singapore Airlines from here.

  1. Group size: Travelers flying in economy class should have at least 10 flyers. For Premium Economy and Business Class, at least 7 passengers are needed. A minimum of 5 passengers is required for first class/suites.
  2. Children and infants: Children between 2 and 12 years may be charged around 75% of the adult fare. Infants under 2 years may be charged around 10% of the fare.
  3. Quote validity: If the journey is more than 30 days away, the price quote stays valid for 3 days. For trips 4-30 days away, it is valid for 24 hours. If departure is within 1-3 days, the quote is valid for only 3 hours.
  4. Flight date changes: Travel dates can be changed if seats are available. Extra fare or any rebooking charges may apply.
  5. Cancellation: Before paying the deposit, the group seats can generally be released without a cancellation fee. Once the deposit is paid, cancellation charges may apply.
  6. Passenger names: Names are usually required around 42 days before departure. Name corrections or changes may be possible with additional charges.
  7. Refunds: When the booking is cancelled within 42 days of departure, the amount may be non-refundable depending on the booking terms.
  8. Name changes: Travelers may have to pay around $67 for name correction. But a complete name change may cost around $134.

How Can I Book a Group Flight with Singapore Airlines?

On Singapore Airlines, a group flight can be booked when all the travel essentials have been met. Moreover, travelers here can contact the airline's desk to reserve a large block of seats on the same flight. Further, the contact number to request a Singapore Airlines group quote is +1 833 727-0118. Travelers can call this number anytime during the day and share the requested details about their travel and group. After collecting and reviewing the request, the price quotation for completing the booking will be shared by the airline.

Why Choose Singapore Airlines for Group Booking?

Travelers can rely on Singapore Airlines when they want to arrange group trips. This is because the airline offers a group booking option that accommodates numerous flyers at once in one flight segment. On top of that, travelers obtain Singapore Airlines group rates with many other added perks. These offerings encourage flyers to choose Singapore Airlines for group booking. If you would like to know what those advantages are, look below.

  1. Travelers can change their travel date and flight for free.
  2. Seats can be secured by paying a 10% deposit.
  3. Name changes are also allowed, but they might incur charges.
  4. Flexibility in submitting names of group members.
