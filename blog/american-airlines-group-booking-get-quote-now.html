American Airlines Group Booking | Get Quote Now

Group Travel Airlines

Journeys curated on American Airlines, the largest air carrier in the world, will be captivating. This airline is known for offering a number of travel options, and one of them is group booking. It allows families, colleagues, friends, or other known companions to fly together. Furthermore, seats for several passengers through the American Airlines group booking feature do not need to be purchased separately; all can be booked under one itinerary. Besides, both domestic and international travelers can benefit from this travel facility of the airline. Nevertheless, they have to adhere to the complete process of group trips, and they can find the same herein.

Does American Airlines Do Group Rates?

Absolutely! American Airlines does offer group rates. These are negotiable fares that the airline offers to flyers to fill the empty seats in bulk without a hitch. Mostly, American Airlines group rates can be acquired by flying during the off-seasons and on weekdays. Other than that, travelers purchasing 10 or more seats together through a single booking request in advance might unlock these discounted fares. Therefore, benefiting from group rates is easier on American Airlines, but considering the important guidelines is also important.

How Can I Book a Group Flight with American Airlines?

American Airlines allows travelers to fly on its flights for different events. They also let groups fly together on its flights for leisure, business, or other travel purposes. But the process of booking an American Airlines group flight is not similar to individual ticketing. To fly in larger numbers, one has to request a quotation, and they can do so by submitting an online form via the group portal of the airline. Those who are interested in learning the steps of this online group reservation method can take a look below.

Access Group Travel: Head to American Airlines' official webpage.

  1. Find the "Business travel services" section.
  2. After that, look for the "Group & Meeting Travel" option.
  3. Then, go to "Request a group rate" to start the process.
  4. Further, enter the necessary details about the travel and send the inquiry to the airline.

Secure the Group Booking: Soon, the airline will forward the quotation for acceptance before it expires.

  1. Follow the payment terms by submitting the advance amount or settling the entire fare.
  2. Next, provide the passenger list along with each traveler’s required information.
  3. The airline can then deliver the e-tickets to the designated group leader’s email address.

How Do I Call American Airlines for Group Flight Booking?

At American Airlines, an array of channels are offered for requesting a group flight. Among them, connecting with the airline by phone is considered an efficient and secure option. In this method, travelers have to ask for an American Airlines group quote by providing their necessary travel information at 800-221-2255. But they might only receive the price quotation when they are eligible to fly together as per the airline’s terms. So, before making a call to book a group flight, make sure to abide by the necessary booking conditions.
