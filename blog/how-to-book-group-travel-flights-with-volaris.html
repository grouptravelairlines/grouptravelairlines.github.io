How to Book Group Travel Flights with Volaris?

Group Travel Airlines

Taking flights to a destination with companions could uplift the travel experience. Creating an itinerary for a travel plan like this feels impeccably smooth with Volaris. It is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline that offers an unrivaled travel option for group travelers with cost-efficient pricing. With the Volaris Airlines group booking, several members could be included under the same itinerary. Not only that, they get to enjoy a host of ancillary services that are solely made available in a shared itinerary. Also, they can get discounts that are made to ease the strain on the travelers' budget. Besides, to get a complete grasp of the facts related to the group travel features of Volaris, take a look at the subheadings below.

Does Volaris Offer Group Discounts?

Unquestionably, group discounts are offered on Volaris. These discounts are one of the special features that allow travelers to fly with a larger group without breaking the bank. Furthermore, the Volaris Airlines group discount is provided to those flyers who maintain the minimum threshold of 10 members. By fulfilling this and some other conditions, flyers can easily avail these enticing airfares. On top of the cheaper airfare, they can also get advantages such as personalized services during the journey, payment flexibility, and more.

How Can I Book a Group Flight with Volaris Airlines?

Arranging seats for a larger group becomes streamlined on Volaris Airlines. Here, travelers are provided with easy online options that make booking for multiple passengers more manageable. To initiate the reservation process to secure a Volaris group flight ticket, one needs to visit its official website. Further, they need to fill out the available form with necessary details to get a quote. Besides, the complete ticketing procedure in online mode is laid out below to give flyers a comprehensive understanding.

  1. Go to the official portal of Volaris: “https://groups.volaris.com/”.
  2. Tap on the “Click here” link to sign up and enter the required details.
  3. Now, paste the link from the email into the browser.
  4. Then enter the email ID and password and log in.
  5. Later, click on the “New booking” icon and choose the "Ad hoc group request."
  6. A form will appear on the screen that needs to be filled out with the necessary information.
  7. Next, select the flights, and an ID will be issued for the travelers.
  8. Further, choose “Request info” and then “View request."
  9. Scroll to the bottom and choose the “ACCEPT” icon.
  10. Click on “Request info” and select the “Make payment” option.
  11. Then, pay the travel cost using the available method.
  12. Now, to share the flyer's name, choose “Request info” and then “Add guest."
  13. Tap on the “Guest detail" link and submit the names of the passengers.
  14. Finally, the tickets will be delivered to your email ID.
