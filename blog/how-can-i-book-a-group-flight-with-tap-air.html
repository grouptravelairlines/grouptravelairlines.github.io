How Can I Book A Group Flight With TAP Air?

Group Travel Airlines

Expeditions for a group can be scheduled conveniently on TAP Air Portugal. On this airline, a plethora of services and amenities are offered to make these shared voyages smoother. Furthermore, the airline's group booking feature makes it easier for families, friends, and colleagues to organize their travel plans together. So, anyone wishing to travel together under one PNR with their companions can make use of the TAP Air Portugal group booking option and have indelible memories with them. But there is much more to explore about this feature. Furthermore, flyers who want to know more can have a look at the subtitles illustrated below.

What Is Considered Group Booking on TAP Air Portugal?

Group booking on TAP Air Portugal refers to a flight reservation made for a number of flyers traveling together to the same destination. Furthermore, those about to travel for business meetings, family vacations, or other group tours can benefit from this option. This TAP Air Portugal group bookings option lets them manage their flight arrangements collectively rather than making separate individual reservations. But the group should have at least 10 passengers to fly together on any of its flights.

Can I Book a Group Flight With TAP Air Portugal?

Absolutely! A flight for a group with TAP Air Portugal can be booked without any hitch. But when a group has to secure a flight, they have to meet the airline's minimum passenger requirements. When they qualify, the request to get a TAP Air Portugal group quote can be sent online via its official website. This is considered the most comfortable and secure way to initiate the booking process. Furthermore, the request to book a group flight has been listed here.

Navigate to Group Travel Services: Browse the TAP Air Portugal official portal.

  1. Head to "Plan a Trip" and open the "See All" option from the "Book" list.
  2. Find "Traveling in Group" and proceed through the "I'm a Private Customer" section.

Confirm Your Group Flight: Enter your registered email or Miles&GO credentials. First-time users must register before proceeding.

  1. Further, submit the journey requirements in the group request form.
  2. The airline will send a fare proposal with a validity period.
  3. Approve the proposal, settle the payment, and share the details of every passenger.
  4. After processing the information, TAP Air Portugal will deliver the group tickets to the group leader's email.

Does TAP Air Portugal Offer Group Rates?

Definitely! Travelers can benefit from group rates on TAP Air Portugal when they wish to secure multiple flight tickets together. But these TAP Air Portugal group rates may be offered under separate booking conditions, unlike standard individual fares. Besides, route availability, departure dates, and the size of the group may influence the overall fare. Generally, taking a flight operated by the airline and booking it in advance can be better to enjoy the discounted fares.

Conclusion

Thus, with the help of group travel features of TAP Air Portugal, an expedition for multiple passengers to the same destination can be easily planned. Here, one can also get economical fares that are only available for shared voyages. Not only this, but flyers can also secure tickets for TAP Air Portugal group travel by following a few simple steps that are mentioned in the above sections. However, if they have questions about anything related to curating group trips, they can contact the airlines.
