How Can I Book Group Flight on Qatar Airways?

Group Travel Airlines

A compelling journey can be created when it is planned with loved ones. However, organizing these journeys requires a lot of effort to accommodate the needs of each and every companion, which can lead to some chaos. But organizing such a trip is effortless when travelers plan to travel on a Qatar Airways flight. This airline offers exceptional group travel services for such trips that allow multiple flyers to fly together on the same route. When passengers make a Qatar Airways group booking, they can also enjoy various perks and amenities. However, these offering of the airline comes with its own stipulations and details about those are gained form the bottom section.

Does Qatar Airways Do Group Booking?

Absolutely yes! Qatar Airways does provide group booking features to flyers. With this, multiple companions can fly together using a single itinerary on the same flight. Moreover, this Qatar Airways group ticket is provided at a much more reasonable rate, which is cheaper than making individual bookings. Furthermore, passengers can also take advantage of its incredible deals and premium offerings by complying with the stipulations.

How To Book Group Flights on Qatar Airways?

On Qatar Airways, planning air travel with numerous passengers fills the journey with a lot of fun and adventurous moments. Here, travelers can reserve their collective trip by sending a Qatar Airways group booking request to their official email address, which is usagroups@us.qatarairways.com. To do so, the flyers have to share the travel details and complete the payment as per the provided quote. Apart from this, the email can also be used to ask any queries or doubts related to the booking and for further specific requirements as well.

What Are the Benefits of Group Travel on Qatar Airways?

Air travel with a large number of flyers on Qatar Airways provides several benefits that make the whole journey memorable. These exclusive perks make the journey seamless and enjoyable from the start of the trip to the end of arrival. However, to acquire Qatar Airways group travel perks its require a minimum of ten members to fly together. Thus, to understand all its perks, one can go through the listed points below.

  1. Get discounted fares based on certain factors.
  2. Dedicated assistance from the airline staff throughout the whole trip.
  3. Flyers have the option to secure a booking by paying a nominal fare at the time of reservation and pay the remaining amount up to 10 days before departure.
  4. Passengers have the option to get the extra baggage allowance before the departure date.
