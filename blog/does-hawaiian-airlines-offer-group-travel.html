Does Hawaiian Airlines Offer Group Travel?

Group Travel Airlines

Setting off on an escape with familiar faces on a Hawaiian Airlines flight adds a special charm to the journey. They connect travelers with various destinations to fly with their companions and offer them bespoke services and features. Furthermore, its group travel facility offers a distinct way to plan their flight journey with known members going to a similar place. Through Hawaiian Airlines group travel, passengers traveling under one itinerary also get access to competitive fares that reduce their travel expenses. Besides, this service has its own features that distinguish it from individual travel agreements. The following information explores the service and its key aspects in greater detail.

Does Hawaiian Airlines Offer Discounts on Group Booking?

Definitely! Travelers flying together with Hawaiian Airlines may qualify for a group discount. With this benefit, eligible flyers may have to pay less than standard individual ticket reservation prices. But Hawaiian Airlines group rates are generally given to those flyers flying with more than 9 passengers under the same PNR. Beyond that, based on place of travel, dates, and availability, these offered discounts can change. So, one has to check these factors to benefit from these special fares before confirming the reservation.

Can I Book a Group Flight with Hawaiian Airlines?

Indeed! Flight tickets for a group on Hawaiian Airlines can be booked. When a number of known flyers plan to fly to the same destination, the airline provides a seamless online option to inquire about booking flights together. Through its official website, they can submit a request based on their group size and planned journey. This Hawaiian Airlines group reservations option also gives passengers a way to explore the available booking terms before moving ahead with their plans. The details of submitting such requests through the website are explained below.

Send Your Group Inquiry: Start by heading to the Hawaiian Airlines official website and accessing its "Menu" icon.

  1. Head to the “Book” section and locate the "Group travel" option.
  2. Further, tap “Request Group Travel” to bring up the booking inquiry.
  3. Then, describe the planned journey by adding the requested travel and contact details in the form, then send the inquiry.

Accept Quote & Finalize Group Booking: The airline will email a fare proposal for the requested trip.

  1. Check its details before the offer expires and accept accordingly.
  2. Afterwards, follow the payment schedule mentioned in the quotation to secure the reservation.
  3. Later on, send the passenger list with names and get the confirmed tickets by email.

Why Choose Hawaiian Airlines for Group Travel?

Flying with a group of individuals on Hawaiian Airlines can bring unmatched perks that are not always available with separate reservations. But eligible groups may only get access to these added advantages. These Hawaiian Airlines group booking benefits make air travel from boarding to landing intriguing and enriching. But the available advantages depend on the group’s itinerary and booking conditions. Additionally, some of the perks of flying as a group on Hawaiian Airlines have been discussed below in detail.

  1. Group members can secure seats on the planned flight under one reservation.
  2. Eligible travelers may collect Atmos Reward Points through the airline’s loyalty program.
  3. Competitive fares are offered by the airline to groups.
  4. Seats by group can be secured early, around 11 months before the scheduled excursion.

Conclusion

So, travelers can choose Hawaiian Airlines to orchestrate their shared journeys. This airline offers group service on all of its operated routes, making it easier for travelers to fly from different regions together. On top of that, one can send a Hawaiian Airlines group request in an effortless way by following the steps shared above. Also, they will be able to become acquainted with the relevant policy and benefits attached to group travel by looking there.
