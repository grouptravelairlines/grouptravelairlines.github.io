Taking a flight to a destination with several companions will give travelers an advantage over the traditional way. How Does Delta Group Travel Work?

On Delta Airlines, group journeys are allowed to be arranged by the passengers. But the facility works under certain conditions that need to be fulfilled by the travelers. Furthermore, by following the Delta group booking policy, one can also request a quote easily and can even avail exciting deals. In this way, individuals would be able to enjoy a cost-effective trip without compromising on their needs. In addition to that, to know about its terms, passengers must refer to the below-mentioned pointers.

  1. Book with a certain group size: Travelers can book seats for the entire group easily only if the passenger count is ten or more.
  2. Fulfilment of conditions: It is necessary to meet the minimum conditions of the airline, and any failure to do so would lead to cancellation of the booking, and the deposit will be forfeited as well.
  3. Group leader: A group must nominate a leader or a point of contact through which the bookings will be requested, and other details will be communicated.
  4. Cancellation policy: In case the booking has to be cancelled, passengers have to pay an applicable fee.

How Can I Contact Delta Group Travel?

Reserving group tickets on Delta Airlines is convenient and straightforward for flyers. This is because the airline provides a number of ways to book a group ticket, and those include either by phone call or by visiting their online portal. Travelers can choose either mode that perfectly suits their preference. By opting for the calling mode, they can dial the Delta group booking phone number, which is 800-532-4777, and they can communicate easily with the help desk representative and inquire about group rates and other information while making reservations. Rather than that, seats can be secured by going through the visit the official website of the airline and the steps to do so are provided on the below points.

  1. Open the official website of Delta Airlines.
  2. Hover over the “Need Help?” icon and select the “Special Circumstances” option.
  3. Now, click on “Get Assistance” under the “Group Travel” tab.
  4. Then, tap on the “Book Group Travel Now” button, and a form will appear.
  5. Enter the details required in each section and submit the form.
  6. Next, receive the quotation from the airline in your email.
  7. Confirm the offer before it ends and pay the travel amount stated therein.
  8. Share the names of the passengers, and tickets will be sent to the designated email ID.
