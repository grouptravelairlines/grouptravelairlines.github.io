How Do I Book Aer Lingus Group Flights?

Group Travel Airlines

Excursions arranged with the national carrier of Ireland, Aer Lingus, will be a pleasant experience. The carrier offers distinct services that aid flyers on the journey, either expediting solo or with known ones. The airline provides group travel services to cater to the needs of large parties intending to fly together. Not only that, but an Aer Lingus group travel can be booked within a single travel plan without having the need to arrange separate plans for each flyer. It reduces the time and effort and increases the satisfaction of the flyers through its services. Besides, to curate a pleasant journey with their loved ones, one needs to be familiar with the topic and learn about the terms and conditions of the service. So read ahead to grab more information about it and have a delightful journey.

What Is the Group Travel Policy on Aer Lingus?

Traveling with companions is effortless on Aer Lingus with its group travel feature. But to acquire the facility, one needs to adhere to the group flying conditions of the airline. Gradually, it becomes easier for passengers to submit an Aer Lingus group quote request by acknowledging these stipulations. Furthermore, by following the rules, it would also become simple for travelers to access the additional perks and offerings that come along with the service. For more details about the policies, refer to the pointers given below.

  1. To utilize the group travel service, there should be seven or more passengers expediting together.
  2. The airline does not offer any child discount on group bookings.
  3. Passengers must submit the names of all individuals at least ten days before the scheduled departure.
  4. The total fare is required to be paid by the travelers at the time of booking.

Can I Book a Group Flight with Aer Lingus?

Certainly! Travelers can secure tickets for their shared excursion readily on Aer Lingus. The carrier offers sophisticated ways to procure the tickets under a centralized system, which aids in fostering coordination and management among the companions. Aside from this, to secure the seats, one can use the online booking approach. But if the number of flyers is more than 25, then the Aer Lingus group number and email can be used, which are (516) 622-4045 and groupsdirect@aerlingus.com, respectively. To learn about the online booking procedure, check the steps listed below.

  1. Navigate to the official website of Aer Lingus to proceed.
  2. Then, click on the “Book” tab and select the “Groups” option from there.
  3. Now, under the “Instant option,” choose the “Book online” button to continue.
  4. Next, submit the required details about the trip and get a quote from the airline.
  5. Approve it before the deadline ends and complete the payment process.
  6. Once done, share the list of names of all flyers traveling in a group.
  7. Finally, the carrier will release the confirmed travel documents to the registered email address of the applicant.

Does Aer Lingus Offer Group Discounts?

Absolutely, Individuals can obtain discounts on securing getaways with a group on Aer Lingus. These special rates aid flyers in having a cost-effective journey with companions without worrying about their pockets. However, an Aer Lingus group booking discount is contingent upon certain aspects such as the number of individuals traveling together, availability, route, and ticket or fare type. So one should check these factors first before booking the tickets.

Conclusion

In brief, traveling with a number of flyers is a square deal on Aer Lingus. This is due to its group travel feature, through which one can find easy ways to book tickets at reasonable rates. Not only that, but by procuring an Aer Lingus group booking, individuals can also unlock several benefits that come along with the service. However, they need to comply with the stipulations that can be found in the above-mentioned titles, along with other essential insights.
