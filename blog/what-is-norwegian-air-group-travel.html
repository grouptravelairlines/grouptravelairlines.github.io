What Is Norwegian Air Group Travel?

Group Travel Airlines

An enjoyable air journey begins with the airline that takes the needs of travelers into consideration. Norwegian Air offers travel options for different purposes, including trips where a number of flyers wish to reach a destination together. But managing such journeys requires a different approach from individual ones. By requesting a Norwegian Air group flight, one can fly together with multiple companions under a common travel plan. Nevertheless, this option may come with specific features and requirements. These are worth understanding before proceeding with a booking. For a closer look at the important aspects of group travel, take a look at the following sections.

Are There Group Discounts on Norwegian Air?

Absolutely! Group discounts are available on Norwegian Air. A traveler can acquire these special rates when they are part of a larger group. Generally, Norwegian Air group travel has to be planned for 10 or more adults to benefit from these discounted fares. Other than that, the timing of booking, chosen routes, availability, and similar factors play an important role in offering group discounts. So, it could be better to take a look at these essentials before seeking group offers.

Can I Book a Group Flight with Norwegian Airlines?

Yes! Travelers can book a group flight with Norwegian Airlines. They can do so by heading to the airline's official website and sharing the essential information through the form. Also, those opting for this Norwegian Air group booking channel get to view essential details about flying together. Through this, flyers can stay prepared with the requirements for making the reservation. But when an adequate number of flyers are included in the trip, then group flights can be booked with this method. Furthermore, one has to follow certain steps to complete their reservation via the website, which are as follows.

  1. Head to the Norwegian Air official website to begin the group reservation.
  2. The homepage "Menu" contains a "Book" section; choose it.
  3. Further, navigate to the "Make a group booking" icon and select it again.
  4. Then, opt for the outbound and return flights, travel dates, and flight type.
  5. Now, hover over "Start reservation" and add the group contact and trip details.
  6. Later, submit the inquiry using "Send request" and obtain an offer by email.
  7. Next, accept it before the deadline and pay the required amount.
  8. In the end, send all passenger names and receive the itinerary by email after confirmation.

Why Choose Norwegian Air Group Flights?

Planning tours for personal and professional events with companions is smoother with Norwegian Air. Here, such journeys are also proven to be economical due to the special fare. Not only that, Norwegian Air group bookings made with more than 9 flyers come with benefits that are exclusive and extraordinary. A traveler can view information about those perks from the cited points.

  1. The fare can be settled in parts within the airline's payment deadlines.
  2. Details of flyers can be changed before the first flight.
  3. Every traveler can carry two checked bags weighing 23 kg each.
  4. The list of final passengers can be provided up to five days before takeoff.
  5. Credit card and invoice payments are available in eligible markets.
  6. On selected international flights, pre-ordered meals are available.

Conclusion

Hence, opting for group features of Norwegian Air can be a worthwhile choice when several passengers want to fly for the same events. Additionally, those arranging their escape using it could acquire countless perks and features. But to make a Norwegian Airlines group request, flyers also have to adhere to some essential conditions. With these, they can curate their group itinerary with more ease.
