How Can I Make a Group Booking on Volotea?

Group Travel Airlines

Excursions planned on Volotea are designed to be an enchanting and convenient travel experience. This low-cost carrier allows expeditions to be organized in both domestic and international regions. No matter whether passengers are traveling individually or in a group, both get to enjoy sophisticated services along the way. Here, to cater for the shared trip, a special group travel service is provided that manages itineraries of numerous members collectively. Additionally, the Volotea group booking feature is considerably more effective than regular expeditions, as it provides cheaper rates and add-on services. Besides this, a delightful trip with peers can be designed by thoroughly reading the sections outlined below.

What Are the Group Travel Terms and Conditions of Volotea?

Executing trips with large crowds on Volotea requires flyers to adhere to the group booking conditions first. Fulfilling these guidelines allows passengers to access the service conveniently without facing any complications. Individuals can also make a Volotea group request easily and can bypass the complexities as well. On top of that, by following the terms and conditions, they will also be able to get the discounted rates and premium perks that are designed especially for them. So, to know more about it, check the pointers illustrated below.

  1. There must be more than nine flyers present in the group to utilize the group service.
  2. The airline allows modifications to the details up to a certain period of time.
  3. Passengers can make changes to the names of the individuals for free till five days before the scheduled departure.
  4. Baggage allowance is provided to flyers when making reservations with large parties.

How to Make a Group Booking on Volotea?

On Volotea, reservations for the entire group can be made swiftly with the procedures the carrier provides. The airline allows passengers to reserve seats under a unified system, which ensures coordination and management among group members. Aside from this, one can make reservations for Volotea groups in several ways. Such methods include reaching out to the airline by calling +34 93 122 21 47, writing an email to groups@volotea.com, and by using the online portal of the carrier. Furthermore, to learn about the online booking approach, refer to the steps outlined below and get better clarity about it.

  1. Navigate to the Volotea official web portal to begin the reservation process.
  2. Then, select the “Groups” option from the "Flights" dropdown and log in using the credentials.
  3. Once done, mention the required details about the trip and get a quote based on that information.
  4. Review and approve it before the given timeframe expires, and move towards finalizing the payment procedure.
  5. Afterwards, share the list of all flyers' names before the due date passes and get the final travel documents to the applicant’s registered email address.

Does Volotea Offer Group Rates?

Indeed! Travelers are entitled to receive group rates when taking flights with companions on Volotea. The carrier provides these special and reduced rates to flyers to make their journey inexpensive. Furthermore, these Volotea group rates also ensure that travelers don’t compromise on their comfort due to high costs and let them enjoy the added privileges without stretching their pockets. This also elevates their overall travel experience and gives them a peaceful trip with known faces.
