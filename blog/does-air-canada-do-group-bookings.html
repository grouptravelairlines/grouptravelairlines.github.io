Does Air Canada Do Group Bookings?

Group Travel Airlines

Journeys arranged within a common travel plan that includes several passengers can be exceptionally well planned with Air Canada. Here, flyers are provided with the group travel facility, which makes it convenient for them to accomplish such a trip. Not only that, but by making an Air Canada group booking, one can secure many additional perks as well. Here, travelers can get added discounts on their tickets that make the journey much more economical for every member traveling in the group. However, these offers and amenities can be achieved when the passengers adhere to the group flying conditions of the airline. To find out a detailed explanation of the group booking service of Air Canada, have a glance at the below-mentioned titles.

What Is Considered a Group Booking with Air Canada?

A group booking on Air Canada refers to a reservation that includes a number of flyers under one travel credential. However, travelers must fly while meeting the minimum conditions set by the airline to avail the advantages of this feature and also have to follow several other guidelines. Once these policies are met, it becomes easier for an individual to raise a request for an Air Canada group quote with no complexities. Additionally, one can even secure lower ticket prices and many other perks as well while adhering to the guidelines. That’s why it is important for one to check the below-mentioned pointers to learn more about the rules.

  1. Total passengers: Bookings can be made when there are more than nine flyers in the group.
  2. Available discounts: One is allowed to get discounted rates if the reservation is made 11 months prior to the scheduled departure.
  3. Seat selection: Passengers are permitted to make group seat selections while booking the tickets at zero additional cost.
  4. Baggage policy: The carrier provides one checked bag to every individual present in the group.

How Do I Book Group Travel with Air Canada?

Securing reservations for group travel on Air Canada is a simple procedure for flyers. The airline offers several modes to secure the reservation based on the travel preferences. Moreover, travelers who are traveling for a leisure trip can book Air Canada group travel by dialling 1-888-247-2262. Additionally, flyers looking to travel in a group for business purposes can request the same by drafting an email to meetings.events@aircanada.ca or requesting it directly from the official website of the airline. Additionally, the detailed procedure to book a group ticket on Air Canada online is explained below.

  1. Start by navigating the official web portal of Air Canada.
  2. On the home screen, you must click on the “More info” tab and then select the “Business and group travel” feature.
  3. Then, choose the “Meetings and events” option.
  4. Now, press the “Register your event” button and make the registrations by filling out the form.
  5. Once submitted, the airline will provide a group quote that must be accepted within the prescribed timeline.
  6. After that, pay the required fare amount and submit the name list of each passenger.
  7. Finally, the confirmed trip documents must be collected by the travelers from the applicant.

Conclusion

Therefore, on Air Canada, it is feasible for flyers to design a seamless shared trip with companions. Such journeys can be accomplished smoothly with the group travel feature of the airline. By choosing this option for the trip, they can also acquire Air Canada group rates that help them to fly without stretching their budget. However, to avail the benefits of these, flyers need to fulfill the terms and conditions set by the airline and details about those are mentioned above. Furthermore, one can also find details about the booking procedure on the same page.
